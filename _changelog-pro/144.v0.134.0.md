## 0.134.0

### November 20, 2026

### &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;🚀 Features

- **unit**: New `unit-labels` prop for units `Intl.NumberFormat` doesn't support — tons, trucks, pallets… e.g. `unit="ton" :unit-labels="{ short: 't', long: { one: 'ton', other: 'tons' } }"` displays `1,234.5 t` (long labels are pluralized for the locale). Typing, decimals and min/max work as with built-in units. [Docs](https://formkit.com/inputs/unit#custom-units) &nbsp;-&nbsp; by @justin-schroeder in https://github.com/formkit/formkit/issues/1484 [<samp>(7c39f)</samp>](https://github.com/formkit/pro/commit/7c39fe19)
- **slider, rating**: Right-to-left support. In `dir="rtl"` forms the slider's minimum sits at the inline-start edge, dragging and pointer math are mirrored, and Left/Right arrows follow the visual direction; rating fills half-stars from the right and defaults `right-to-left` from the page direction (an explicit prop still wins). LTR rendering and events are unchanged. Pairs with RTL support in `@formkit/theme-regenesis` 2.1.0. &nbsp;-&nbsp; by @justin-schroeder in https://github.com/formkit/formkit/issues/1827 [<samp>(b403b)</samp>](https://github.com/formkit/pro/commit/b403b211)
