## 0.132.0

### November 18, 2026

### &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;🚀 Features

- **taglist, dropdown, autocomplete**: New `allow-duplicates` prop lets the same option be selected more than once (`taglist`, or `dropdown`/`autocomplete` with `multiple`). Every pick adds another occurrence, the listbox stops marking options as selected, removing a tag removes just that one occurrence (click, Backspace or keyboard), `max` counts occurrences, and drag-to-reorder works with duplicates. Off by default — no change unless you opt in. [Docs](https://formkit.com/inputs/taglist#allow-duplicates) &nbsp;-&nbsp; by @justin-schroeder in https://github.com/formkit/formkit/issues/1574 [<samp>(8490c)</samp>](https://github.com/formkit/pro/commit/8490c3bf)

### &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;🐞 Bug Fixes

- **rating, colorpicker**: The label no longer points `for` at a missing id (rating) or a `<div>` (colorpicker) — Chrome flagged it as "Incorrect use of `<label for=FORM_ELEMENT>`". Both are now labelled via `aria-labelledby`, and rating's items are a `radiogroup`. &nbsp;-&nbsp; by @justin-schroeder in https://github.com/formkit/formkit/issues/1147 [<samp>(64a2d)</samp>](https://github.com/formkit/pro/commit/64a2d62e)
