## 0.133.0

### November 18, 2026

### &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;🚀 Features

- **datepicker**: New `token-placeholders` prop shows custom characters for unfilled format tokens — e.g. `:token-placeholders="{ DD: 'TT', YYYY: 'JJJJ' }"` displays `TT.MM.JJJJ` for German users. Typing works exactly as before. [Docs](https://formkit.com/inputs/datepicker#token-placeholders) &nbsp;-&nbsp; by @justin-schroeder in https://github.com/formkit/formkit/issues/1165 [<samp>(e7cb0)</samp>](https://github.com/formkit/pro/commit/e7cb0836)

### &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;🐞 Bug Fixes

- **datepicker, mask**: Keystrokes typed immediately after focus, or with no delay between keys, are no longer dropped or misplaced (`17052024` could become `05.02.YYYY`). This matters for browser agents, autofill, barcode scanners and test runners, which all type instantly. Three timer-based selection races in the mask are fixed; human-speed typing and mouse behavior are unchanged. &nbsp;-&nbsp; by @justin-schroeder in https://github.com/formkit/formkit/issues/1826 [<samp>(d21c6)</samp>](https://github.com/formkit/pro/commit/d21c6e18)
- **dropdown, autocomplete, taglist**: Fixed a leaked `document` click listener when an input unmounted within ~10ms of opening its listbox. &nbsp;-&nbsp; by @justin-schroeder [<samp>(b8f93)</samp>](https://github.com/formkit/pro/commit/b8f935d2)
