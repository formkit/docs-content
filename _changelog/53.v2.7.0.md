## 2.7.0

### November 18, 2026

### &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;✨ Highlights

- **Tailwind prefixes, finally** 🎨 — `npx formkit theme --prefix tw` (Tailwind v4, `prefix(tw)`) or `--prefix tw-` (v3, `prefix: 'tw-'`) prefixes every class in your generated theme, and the prefix sticks when you re-run the CLI to edit it. Works with themes.formkit.com and local themes. (#1157)
- **`node.closest()`** 🧭 — the upward counterpart of `find()`, modeled on `Element.closest()`: `node.closest('address')`, `node.closest('list', 'type')`, or a search function. Also usable in addresses: `node.at('$self.closest(list, type).0.price')`. (#1198)
